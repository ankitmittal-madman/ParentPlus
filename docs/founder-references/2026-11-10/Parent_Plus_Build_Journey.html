How we'll work together

You stay in charge of what we build and when it's ready. I do the building, and explain every step in plain words. You never need to read code.

You, the founder

  • Decide priorities, scope and anything to do with money or families' data
  • Prepare accounts and pilot material (Stage 0)
  • Try every preview link and tell me what you expected
  • Approve each gate before we move on

Me, your AI co-founder

  • Plan each stage: what, why, demo script and gate
  • Build in small, tested pieces, to the standards of top product teams
  • Send a plain-language build diary after every session
  • Stop and ask before spending money, changing what data we collect, or skipping a check
